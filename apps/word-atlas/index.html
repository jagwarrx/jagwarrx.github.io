Word Atlas

One word across 24 major languages, drawn as a map. Click any word for where it came from and when people use it.

Open full screen (best on a laptop)

What it shows

  • The closest word in each language. The word you type sits in the middle and each language holds one spoke. Words in other scripts carry a transliteration, so 家 reads jiā.
  • Nearby words. Past each word sit the phrases and near matches that language has. A diamond marks the ones with no match in most other languages.
  • Origin and use. Click a word for where it came from and the context people use it in. From there you can open the related words in that language.
  • Language families. The outer ring names the family each language belongs to.

How to use it

  • The example word, home, opens without a key. Click around it first.
  • A new word needs your own Claude API key. The key stays in your browser and goes only to Anthropic.
  • Under the search box you can pick the languages and choose faster or more careful answers.

What it gets wrong

  • Claude writes the entries from memory. Etymologies, examples and claims that a word has no match elsewhere can be wrong, so check a dictionary before you quote one.
