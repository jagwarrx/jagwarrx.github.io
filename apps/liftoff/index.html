Liftoff Tracker

A strength and running programme tracker: form cues for every lift, a rest timer, and weights that go up when you hit every rep.

How it works

  • Pick a programme, then tap through each set as you lift.
  • The weight goes up when you hit every rep; three misses in a row drop it 10%.
  • Each lift has form cues and an animated figure you can turn.

Data stays in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
