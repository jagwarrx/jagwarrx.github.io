Baby tracker

Feeds, sleep and diapers in a tap or two, with a reminder when the next feed is due. Made for the first weeks with our newborn.

How it works

  • Start a feed or a sleep with one tap; tap again to stop.
  • Home shows when the next feed is due.
  • Export everything as a CSV, or import one.

Data stays in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
