Emoji images from Noto Emoji by Google (https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji), the Android emoji set.
The images are licensed under the Apache License 2.0 (LICENSE.txt). Only images are used here, none of that repo's fonts.
Files are the 2D/svg images, renamed u<codepoint>.svg and minified with svgo.
u-pyramid.svg, u-stupa.svg, u-wheel.svg, u-anchor.svg and u-plane.svg are drawn for this site in the Noto style (Noto has no pyramid, stupa or spoked wheel; the anchor marks sea battles, the plane air battles).
