Map data for World History in Eight Regions.

w-3000.json to w2010.json: simplified from historical-basemaps by André Ourednik
(https://github.com/aourednik/historical-basemaps), licensed GPL-3.0
(https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html). These files are a modified version:
simplified with mapshaper, coordinates rounded to 0.1 degree, regions added.
The script that made them is ../map-build.py.

w2026.json and land.json: Natural Earth (public domain), via the world-atlas package.
