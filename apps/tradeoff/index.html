What is the tradeoff?

Name a decision. See what you gain, what you give up and when it is worth it, with a verdict at the end.

How it works

  • Type a decision, like leaving a job to freelance, and press Weigh it.
  • Add your specifics (savings, goals, who depends on you) and the verdict is about you, not about people in general.
  • Both sides come first. The verdict comes last, with a balance that shows which way it leans and a line on whether you can undo the choice.
  • Three worked examples open without a key: starting a company, dropping out of college and publishing a blog.

A new decision needs your own Claude API key. The key stays in your browser and goes only to Anthropic. Your recent decisions are kept in this browser.

Claude writes each answer from general knowledge. Use it to think, and check anything that matters with someone who knows your situation.
