Recommended

Things I found valuable or fun and recommended to friends, collected in 2020 and 2021 on the old site. For books, see Reading+Notes and Lists of Book Lists.

Tools

  • RoamPages within pages, linked every which way. I use it for outlining articles, journalling and as a personal wiki.
  • SunsamaTime-block tasks for daily focus.
  • Go F***ing WorkBrowser extension that blocks websites.
  • ProcreateBest use of an iPad, in my opinion.
  • UnsplashFree, non-copyrighted images.
  • Google ColabBrilliant online Python environment. Write, test and share code with common libraries pre-installed and free hosted GPUs.
  • X in Y minutesThe syntax of every popular programming language, one page each.
  • ScrimbaGreat web development tutorials.

Health

  • Vitamin DDeficiency is very common. I started supplementing after testing low and had an immediate boost in mood and sleep quality.
  • Blue light blocking glassesNoticeable improvement in sleep quality when worn 2 to 3 hours before bed.
  • L-TheanineFound in green tea. Promotes relaxed concentration and cancels the jitters from caffeine.

Articles

  • The Cook and the ChefMy favorite post from my favorite blog. How does Elon Musk do what he does?
  • PrinciplesMore of an e-book, and really, really good. Ray Dalio on why principles matter and what his are.
  • How to Get Rich Without Getting LuckyA tweetstorm by Naval Ravikant. Made me think hard about status seeking, value creation and leverage.
  • The Gervais PrincipleWhat makes The Office so relatable?

Podcasts

  • The Tim Ferriss ShowLong conversations with top athletes, artists, founders and scientists about how they operate.
  • The Big Grande Teacher's LoungeImprovisers pretend to be teachers at an imaginary school. Belly-laugh material.
  • SerialThe podcast that started it all. A gripping documentary-style investigation of a real murder.
  • How to Take Over the WorldHistory podcast on the lives of people like Caesar, Napoleon, the Rothschilds and Steve Jobs.
  • Lex Fridman with Joscha BachOne of the greatest podcast episodes ever, eclipsed only by the next one.
  • Dead Authors Podcast with Andy DalyH.G. Wells interviews dead authors. Improvised, and somehow educational.

YouTube

  • Historia CivilisRoman history told with little colored squares. Better than it sounds.
  • WhatIfAltHistAlternate history and big-picture cultural analysis.
  • Qi GongA short routine I actually kept doing.
  • Key and PeeleFor when nothing else is working.
