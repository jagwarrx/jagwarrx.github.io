Sleep Calculator

Pick a wake-up time, get the bedtimes that land on a full sleep cycle.

Status
archived
Built
August 2021
Stack
React

A small React app. Enter the time you need to be up and it counts back in 90-minute sleep cycles, plus the fifteen minutes it takes most people to fall asleep, and lists the bedtimes that avoid waking mid-cycle.
