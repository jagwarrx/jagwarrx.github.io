About

Who writes this, and what I do.

I'm Hari. For six years I've built software that has to work on real inputs: messy forms, phone calls, résumés, bank documents. These days that means AI agents, and most of my time goes to the parts that don't show up in a demo: the prompt that has to handle 200 form types, the call where someone talks over the agent, and knowing when the agent should hand off to a person.

Outside work I spend an unreasonable amount of time on the systems I use to think, plan and remember. This site is where I write the parts down. Things I keep coming back to: personal operating systems, note-taking that pays off later, chess, markets and voice interfaces.
