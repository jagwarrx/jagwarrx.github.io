The Novelty of Active Recall

November 2026. 271 words.

For an ADHD brain, active recall is novel. Being able to load the context into my mind before the task seems important, but I have to do it just in time.

Recall, not rereading

Rereading my notes is the same input a second time. The brain has seen it, so it slides off. Recall is a question: what do I remember about this? It is a small puzzle with an answer I can check, and that is enough novelty to hold attention for two minutes. Pulling something out of memory also makes it stick better than reading it again.

Why load the context

A task starts cold. Without the context in my head, the first part of the block goes to finding where I was, and that is where the drift starts. Recalling first puts the goal, the last state and the next step in working memory, so the block starts warm.

Why just in time

Working memory doesn’t hold. Load the context the night before, or an hour early, and by the time the block starts something else has pushed it out. The choosing can happen early, the day before. The loading happens at the start of the block, not before.

How

  • Timer on, then 2 minutes of recall. On the scratchpad, before opening anything: the goal, where I left off, the next step.
  • Then check the breadcrumb. The line I left at the end of the last block. What I got wrong or missed is what to look at first.
  • Then start. No more reading.

In Improvised Productivity, the breadcrumb is the cue. Recall is what loads it.
