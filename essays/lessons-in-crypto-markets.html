Lessons In Crypto Markets

August 2021. 876 words.

I've navigated the volatile crypto market for a bit, and have picked up some useful ideas along the way.

With blockchain ecosystems gaining adoption, there is a lot of speculative potential for projects. This creates volatile markets along with periodic bubbles, which makes crypto markets a haven for traders.

I've outlined my ideas around the the market below. They have changed since I got in, and I expect they will continue to evolve. This is by no means financial advice.

What I subscribe to now:

  1. HODL quality coins

    I'm convinced that the highest reward to risk is in entering strong + low market capitalization projects early. Early conviction and patience pays off big time.

    Usually the best opportunities occur where there's where there's a mismatch between the market capitalization of token and the:

    - addressable market
    - total value captured by the project
    - market capitalization of a similar or older project
    - token economics (deflationary tokens)

    Having a strong community, and memes, are also things to look out for.

    There are major themes in crypto - de-fi, layer 2's, nfts - each with their own seasons, where the narrative surrounding them is very bullish. Active traders make money by being a step ahead of the market, and loading up on them when there isn't much hype.

    It's sensible to take profits and exit when the market is driven purely by speculative frenzy, and buy back a larger amount of tokens after the market corrects. There are several indicators that help understand when the market is overextended, especially the fear and greed index. Also paying attention to your own emotions is a signal - when euphoric, start scaling out.

  2. Rotate Profits

    Alt-coins are usually paired against USD, bitcoin and ethereum. While most people focus on the USD pairing, the real action happens against the BTC/ETH pairing of alt-coins. There are periods where alts outperform BTC/ETH and vice versa. These price swings in alt-coins is caused by capital rotation between bitcoin and other coins. After a pump in alt-coins, profit taking ensues and the price of the BTC pair begins to fall.

    A slightly more involved strategy requires taking profits in BTC, riding it until the next mini alt-season (periods in the cycle where alts out-perform bitcoin) and buying back alts for cheap against their BTC pair, and repeating this till the blow off top for the cycle.

    Caveat: Fundamentally strong and undervalued alts don't really care about what part of the cycle it is.

    So keep the majority of portfolio in high conviction coins, and play on the sidelines with seasonal rotations.

Thoughts from earlier in the cycle:

  1. Trading takes time.

    The crypto markets are always open. Timing the market correctly requires a lot of context. Sources of information like twitter are infinity pools and shifting through tons of information takes up a lot of mental bandwidth.

  2. Take profits systematically.

    Remember to think more about protecting your capital than about profits.

  3. Have a plan.

    Without a plan, you are much more likely to get emotional about the trade and end up regretting it (bought/sold too late/early). After closing your trade, ignore what happens afterwards in terms of price action.

  4. Bitcoin is king.

    Bitcoin determines the behavior of the whole crypto market. We may see a decoupling over the next few years, but for now if bitcoin tanks, the market tanks.

    The money has historically flown from bitcoin ➞ ethereum ➞ large cap ➞ microcap.

    During an alt-season, micro-caps pump driven by speculation. After a blow-off top, it comes crashing down, leaving "bag-holders" who bought the top and providing exit liquidity to sellers.

    To know whats actually going on with your portfolio, denominate in BTC (or ETH) as opposed to fiat currency.

  5. Ignore speculative news.

    More often than not, the profitable thing to do is the opposite of what the popular sentiment suggests. Be greedy when others are fearful, and fearful when others are greedy.

    Buy the rumour, sell the news. This requires being one step ahead of the market.

    Consuming information when emotions are running high increases the chances of derailing your plan. It's best to be one step ahead of the crowd, and stay calm when the market panics.

  6. Position sizing is key.

    Calculate a position size in relation to portfolio %. The position size is based on the risk you're willing to take on in relation to the reward of the trade (R:R ratio).

  7. 99% coins are s***-coins.

    Very few coins that have real world adoption and legit use case. The increase in price for most coins is pure speculation, and not because of underlying value. When speculating, the market capitalization of a coin is what matters.

  8. Do Your Own Research

    Know what you're buying. With conviction in a project, you'll be able to manage your emotions better. It's best to buy in when hardly anyone is talking about a project.
  9. Keep a tab on network activity

    Most chains have public on-chain data. Analyzing this can help find wallets that are buying large amount of tokens.

  1. Buy at support/dips.

    Wait for the price to hit a support level before buying. Keep ~30% of portfolio liquid in stable-coins for buying dips.

High level principles:

  • Consolidate based on conviction
  • Compound/Rotate based on number of x's
