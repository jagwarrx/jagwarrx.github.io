Lists of Book Lists

February 2021. 196 words.

"Only the Autodidacts Are Free"

I've been getting book recommendations from the internet for over a decade now.

Here are some of the best book lists I've found.

Ribbonfarm

A unique website, with mind expanding reads. Has a wonderful book list, and the articles make more sense after reading a few of the recommended books. Discovered John Boyd and "Tempo" here. Absolute gem.

Books for Entrepreneurs — Taylor Pearson

Possibly the best collection of books for a solo internet entrepreneur. 10/10 book recommendations on finances, mindset, management, marketing, productivity, systems.

Books for Entrepreneurs — Steve Blank

Possibly the best collection of books for a traditional entrepreneur. Steve Blank wrote the "Startup Owner's Manual", the considered the bible of the Startup world. He's seen it all, and recommendations reflect that.

Best Meditation Books — Deconstructing Yourself

Solid list of meditation books from a podcaster who's conducted hundreds of interviews with meditation teachers.

Best Philosophy Books — Jordan Peterson

Jordan Peterson, a philosophy professor, encourages students to sharpen their thinking and be articulate.
